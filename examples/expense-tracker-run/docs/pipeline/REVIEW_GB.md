[N1][human] Success metrics are checked by weaker proxies than the PRD states — evidence: PRD §8 lines 255-256 "ภายใน 30 วินาที … (ทดสอบ usability 3–5 คน)" and "≤ 4 การแตะหลังเปิดฟอร์ม และ ≤ 10 วินาที"; SA_BLUEPRINT §11 row 3 replaces the 30 s test with "≤5 interactions … human test 3–5 users optional" (logged as `[sa][DEVIATION]` in DECISIONS.md); row 2's title says "≤10 s" but its Expected column only has "≤4 interactions", so the 10 s part is never measured. The proxy is disclosed, not hidden, but passing the rows does not prove 30 s or 10 s. The human should accept the proxy or keep the human test at the QA gate.
2. [N2][human] The nine PRD amendments become approved text only when Gate 1 is approved — evidence: docs/pipeline/STATE.md `gate1: pending`; PRD lines tagged `[amend A1]`..`[amend A9]`. If the human rejects any of them, the matching `[PRD-AMEND An]` lines in the blueprint and contract must be realigned.

[N3][human] No backup or export: clearing browser data loses every entry — evidence: SA_BLUEPRINT §13 "Backup: N/A … Clearing browser data loses everything"; PRD §1 non-goals "export/import", PRD §9 Q4 still open; IDEA "ไม่ต้องมี … export".
